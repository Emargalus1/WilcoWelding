WELDING SUPPLY LIST

Bring these items for the Wilco Welding Program.

  • 3-subject notebook
  • Folder
  • Pencil, pen, and highlighter
  • Black Sharpie
  • Light-colored paint marker
  • Flashlight
  • Safety glasses
  • Welding hood
  • Welding gloves
  • Welding jacket
  • Base layer: 100% cotton shirt and jeans
  • Steel-toe or composite-toe boots
