ALUMNI PATHWAYS

COLLEGE & TRAINING

Continue building welding and fabrication skills through technical schools, community colleges, certification programs, and advanced training.

APPRENTICESHIPS

Move into paid, hands-on training opportunities in welding, pipefitting, construction, manufacturing, and related trades.

CAREERS

Use classroom and shop experience to pursue entry-level work and long-term careers in welding, fabrication, maintenance, and manufacturing.
